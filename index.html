About the project

Course, research, and platform — built together

Technical Interviews 101 is being developed through Grasshopper Lab at the University of Florida. Beyond teaching the material, the project studies how AI coding agents can support interview prep, using a validated pre/post survey to measure real learning outcomes.

The end goal: a complete course package, a research-backed assessment tool, and a clear answer to whether AI-assisted teaching actually improves interview readiness.

Delivered on

Edugator.org

A UF-affiliated, LeetCode-style coding platform built for structured practice.
